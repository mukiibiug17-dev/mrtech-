FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers before you get in touch.

What should I do if I think I've been hacked?

Disconnect the affected device from the internet, do not switch it off or delete anything, change your passwords from a different clean device, and contact us. Keeping everything as it is helps preserve evidence.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Every engagement is handled confidentially, and we only access what is needed for the work you ask us to do.

Can your digital forensics work be used in court?

We follow careful evidence-handling practices including a documented chain of custody. Legal requirements differ by country, so we recommend involving your lawyer early.

Do you work with small businesses and individuals?

Yes. We support individuals, small businesses and larger organisations, and tailor our services to your size and budget.

Do you install CCTV cameras and networks?

Yes. We install CCTV camera systems, structured cabling, Wi-Fi and outdoor network connections, and set them up with security in mind so they are not an easy target.
